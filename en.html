About Us

God’s Will Hana Church was planted in obedience to God’s call to prepare for mission to North Korea and for reunification. Our vision is to be one with God, one with each other, and one Korea.

We belong to the Presbyterian Church of Korea (PCK, Tonghap). Our ministries include overseas outreach (Kenya, Ethiopia, Lebanon, the Philippines and Thailand) and weekly care for North Korean defector families in Busan.
